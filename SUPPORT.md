# Support

CrossDrive is **abandoned** and receives no support.

- Issues and pull requests will not be answered, except GPL source-code
  requests (see below).
- There will be no further releases or fixes from the original author.
- Nothing here is a promise that anything works.

## GPL source-code requests

CrossDrive binaries include GPL-covered components. The complete corresponding
source for v1.5.35 is attached to its release. The written offer in
[`build/GPL_SOURCE_OFFER.txt`](build/GPL_SOURCE_OFFER.txt) still applies for its
stated period: request source by opening an issue as described there.

## Want to improve it?

You are welcome to fork the repository and continue the work under the terms of
the [MIT License](LICENSE). Please:

- read [Known dangerous behaviour](README.md#known-dangerous-behaviour) first;
- develop and test against disk images, never against real drives holding data;
- keep the third-party and GPL notices in `build/` and `docs/gpl-source/`
  with any binaries you distribute.

## If CrossDrive damaged a drive

Stop using the drive immediately and do not let any tool "repair" it. Use a
dedicated data-recovery tool, or a data-recovery service, working from a copy of
the disk where possible.
