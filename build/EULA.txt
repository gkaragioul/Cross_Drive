READ BEFORE INSTALLING
----------------------

CrossDrive is an ABANDONED RESEARCH PROJECT. It is not maintained or supported.

CrossDrive reads and writes raw disks and CAN DAMAGE OR DESTROY THE DATA on the
drives you connect to it. In particular, it mounts HFS+ volumes read-write by
default and switches their journal off without replaying it, which can make a
Mac drive unmountable. Only use it on drives you have fully backed up, or on
disk images.
